**Curator's Note**

On June 15, 2156, Sector 7 of Luna University Campus experienced catastrophic decompression. 847 people died in 38 seconds. Among them was Prof. Dr. Benedikt Guth, whose final message—sent two hours before his death—speaks of "the third model" and "the Foundation that has no form."

Four explanations exist for the event. The official report blames material fatigue. An underground press dossier alleges sabotage by Earth Purist terrorists. Survivor testimonies describe premonitions and anomalous phenomena. Guth's message suggests something else entirely—contact with a reality beneath structure itself.

The Archive preserves all four narratives. It does not adjudicate between them.

**[KN-0.1]**

Guth wrote: "The walls hum. 12 Hz. Like in Ceres. Like on Mars. Like everywhere humans build and believe they build alone."

The 12 Hz frequency appears in multiple Archive documents. Mars monoliths emit 108 Hz—a ninth harmonic. The Source in Ceres pulses at 12 Hz. The Resonance Cathedral's natural frequency is 12 Hz. The pattern is too consistent to ignore.

The Archive documents. It does not explain.

— T.P.K.

**Source 1: Official Report**

*Luna Safety Commission Final Report · Published: March 2157*

**Executive Summary**

On June 15, 2156, at 14:17:34 LST, catastrophic structural failure occurred in Pressure Wall 7-Alpha-3, Luna University Campus, Sector 7. The failure initiated a rapid decompression cascade affecting an estimated 340,000 cubic meters of pressurized habitat volume.

**Timeline**

- 14:17:34 — Initial breach detected (Pressure Wall 7-Alpha-3)
- 14:17:41 — Emergency bulkheads fail to deploy (7 seconds delay, cause unknown)
- 14:17:58 — Secondary breaches in adjacent walls (cascading failure)
- 14:18:12 — Sector 7 fully depressurized (38 seconds total)
- 14:22:00 — First rescue teams arrive (cannot enter due to vacuum)

**Cause of Failure**

Metallurgical analysis of recovered wall fragments indicates progressive fatigue fracture in titanium-aluminum alloy support beam 7A3-B14. Microfractures, invisible to standard inspection protocols, propagated over an estimated 8–12 year period.

Contributing factors: age (58 years), thermal cycling stress, inspection gaps (last survey 2151), and maintenance budget cuts (34% reduction 2152–2156).

**Conclusion**

The Commission finds no evidence of sabotage, deliberate negligence, or external attack. The failure resulted from material fatigue exacerbated by inadequate maintenance.

**Dissenting Opinion**

Commissioner Rashida Al-Fahim: "While I concur with the technical analysis, I cannot accept the conclusion. The simultaneous failure of seven emergency bulkheads—designed with triple redundancy—defies statistical probability. The official report does not adequately address this anomaly. I recommend continued investigation."

Status: Report accepted by Luna Governing Council (vote: 8–3). Dissent noted but not pursued.

**Source 2: Sabotage Theory Dossier**

*"The Unasked Questions" — Luna Underground Press, April 2158*

The official report is a whitewash. 847 people died. The Luna Safety Commission closed the case in nine months. We did not.

**Evidence Cluster 1: The Bulkhead Failure**

FACT: Seven emergency bulkheads failed to deploy. OFFICIAL EXPLANATION: Simultaneous electrical fault. PROBABILITY: 0.00034% (1 in 294,000).

All seven bulkheads shared a common control node—Server 7-Delta-9, physically located in Sector 6 (unaffected by decompression). Forensic analysis found anomalous code injection timestamped 48 hours before the event. The analyst, Dr. Shen Zhao, lost university funding three weeks after publishing. He now works on Mars.

**Evidence Cluster 2: Terra Firma**

Between 2153–2156, the radical anti-colonization movement "Terra Firma" conducted seven documented attacks on off-Earth infrastructure, killing 15 people. Their January 2156 manifesto declared: "Humanity's place is Earth. The colonies are hubris."

Three known Terra Firma operatives were on Luna in May 2156. None were questioned about the June event. An anonymous Luna Security source stated: "We were told to let it go. From high up. Very high."

**Evidence Cluster 3: The Convenient Victims**

Of 847 dead, 23 were members of the Luna Independence Movement—3.3× the expected rate for a random event. Among them: Senator Amara Okonkwo (lead independence advocate), Dr. Chen Wei (author of "The Lunar Constitution"), and Prof. Dr. Benedikt Guth (sympathetic moderator).

Conclusion: Someone wanted these voices silenced.

Status: Dismissed as conspiracy theory by authorities.

**Source 3: Survivor Testimonies**

*Selected Accounts (17 survivors, compiled 2156–2162)*

**Testimony 1 — Dr. Lena Kowalski**, Physicist, Luna University — Interviewed June 20, 2156

"I was in Lab 7-C, analyzing spectral data. At 14:15—two minutes before the breach—I heard a sound. Not loud. A hum. Low frequency, maybe 12–15 Hz. It made my chest vibrate. I thought it was the ventilation system. But then the hum changed. It became... rhythmic. Like breathing. I looked at my colleague. He heard it too. We joked: 'The building is alive.' Two minutes later, the wall exploded."

Follow-up: Kowalski reported the sound to the Safety Commission. They classified it as "pre-failure stress resonance." They did not explain why it sounded like breathing.

**Testimony 2 — Miguel Santos**, Maintenance Engineer — Interviewed July 3, 2156

"I survived because I was in the access tunnel. When the alarm went off, I ran toward Sector 6. The bulkhead should have closed behind me. It didn't. I got through. Turned around. The door was still open. I could see people running, screaming. The air was being sucked out. I tried to close it manually. It wouldn't move. Then—I swear to God—it closed on its own. Not slowly. Fast. Like a guillotine. Seven people were in the doorway. They were cut in half."

Follow-up: Santos insists he did not touch the door. "It just... closed."

**Testimony 3 — Dr. Amina Osei**, Psychologist — Interviewed September 12, 2156

"At 14:10, I was in my office. I felt pressure. Not physical. Emotional. Like something was watching me. At 14:15, I walked into the hallway. Three others were there. All pale, sweating. One woman looked at me and said: 'Do you feel it?' I said yes. She said: 'It's coming.' Two minutes later, the wall ruptured."

Follow-up: The woman is not on the survivor list. Not on the casualty list. No identity ever established.

**Testimony 4 — Takeshi Yamamoto**, Linguist, partner of Prof. Dr. Benedikt Guth — Interviewed October 1, 2156

"Benedikt sent me a message two hours before the event. It said: 'Ich habe verstanden. Das dritte Modell ist nicht zwischen ihnen. Es ist unter ihnen.' I didn't understand. I replied: 'Under what?' He never answered. Two hours later, he was dead."

Follow-up: Yamamoto possesses Guth's incomplete notes. The last twelve pages are missing.

**Testimony 5 — Anonymous Survivor**, Structural Engineer — Interviewed May 2162 (6 years post-event)

"I need you to understand: I'm not crazy. I'm a structural engineer. I don't believe in ghosts. But I saw something. In the breach. In the blackness. Something moved. Not a person. Not debris. It was geometric. Angular. Like a shadow with edges. It was there for maybe three seconds. Then it was gone."

Follow-up: "What did it look like?" "Like a triangle. But not flat. Three-dimensional. Rotating. And it was... looking at me."

Status: Classified as hypoxia-induced hallucination.
