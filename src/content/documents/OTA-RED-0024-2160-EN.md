**Curator's Note**

Following the 2147 debate (OTA-CUL-0013-2147-EN), Dr. Demir proposed a follow-up debate for 2160. Nemeth Tiefenborn accepted. Ticket requests exceeded cathedral capacity fortyfold.

The debate was never held. Officially. What follows are three competing accounts of what actually happened — or did not.

— T.P.K.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

**Narrative A: Political Suppression**

By 2159, the Rationalist Builders' Coalition (RBC) and the Symbiotic Cultivators' Movement (SCM) had polarized to the point that the Ceres government classified a second debate as a "societal risk." HeliosCorp allegedly threatened to withdraw investment if the debate proceeded.

Status: Unproven, but consistent with documented HeliosCorp behavior elsewhere in the Archive.

**Narrative B: Personal Decision**

Nemeth declined. A letter to Demir, leaked in 2161, reads in part:

"I have nothing more to say. Whoever could not hear will not hear in 2160 either. Whoever has heard needs no repetition."

Demir accepted the cancellation without public comment.

Status: Authenticity of the letter disputed.

**Narrative C: The Debate Occurred — Privately**

Rumors persist that Demir and Nemeth met privately on Level 6, on 12 March 2160 — exactly thirteen years after the original debate. No recording exists. No witnesses are named. The only "evidence" is an entry attributed to Demir's private journal, never itself published but quoted in a 2175 biography:

"Nemeth was right. And I was right. Benedikt was most right of all: the question was wrong. We wasted thirteen years answering the wrong one."

Status: Source (2175 biography) of disputed reliability.

It notes only that Narrative C, if true, would place Guth's unfinished "third position" (OTA-RED-0016-2160-EN) at the center of a private reconciliation neither party ever publicly acknowledged.
