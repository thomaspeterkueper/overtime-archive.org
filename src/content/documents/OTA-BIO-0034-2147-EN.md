**[KN-0.1]**

Naming note: This individual is documented under the name **Nemeth Tiefenborn**, distinct from the unrelated canonical NOXIA character **Kaelen** (Ceres/Belt, Belt physiology, SYELP Phase 2, they/them). An earlier working draft of this profile used the name "Kaelen Tiefenborn" in error, creating a naming collision within the NOXIA universe. The Archive corrects this here; all references elsewhere should use Nemeth Tiefenborn.

— T.P.K.

**I. Biography**

Nemeth Tiefenborn was the first person born on Level 5 of Ceres Habitat Alpha — a birth marked at the time as a milestone of the colony. Parents: Mira Tiefenborn (geologist, d. 2138) and an unregistered father.

Nemeth received no formal university education, learning instead from "the Elders" — first-generation Ceres settlers. At 16, Nemeth reported a mystical experience in the chamber known as the Whispering Room, describing it as hearing "the voice of the stone." At 19, Nemeth joined the Order of the Silent Word, then twelve members strong.

**II. Personal**

Nemeth lives in an 8m² cell on Level 6, adjacent to the Source chamber. Vegetarian by spiritual conviction. Speaks slowly, with long pauses — a manner some attribute to "Deep-Dweller speech." Has not visited Level 1 since 2133 (age 14): "The surface is loud."

Uses they/them pronouns in official Ceres records.

**III. The 2144 Accusation**

In 2145, Nemeth was accused of "indoctrinating" young recruits of the Order; the charge was dropped. Rationalists have called Nemeth a charlatan; Cultivators, a prophet.

**IV. The 2151 Disappearance**

In 2151, Nemeth vanished for three months into uncharted tunnels in Sector 7 — the same sector documented by Dr. Aris Thorne as exhibiting unplanned tunnel growth. Nemeth returned changed, and has not spoken publicly of what was found there.

**[KN-4.1]**

The timing coincides with the final weeks of Prof. Dr. Benedikt Guth's work on "the third model" (see OTA-RED-0016-2160-EN). Whether these two threads intersect remains, at this archival date, undetermined. The Archive notes the coincidence.

— T.P.K.

**V. Later Life**

In 2149, Nemeth founded the School of Deep Listening on Level 6. In 2155, Nemeth published *"Silence Has No Words"* — a book consisting entirely of blank pages, which nonetheless sold two million copies (see OTA-RED-0025-2155-EN, pending).

As of this archival date, Nemeth — 79 years old — continues to live on Level 6, reportedly "deeper than before."
