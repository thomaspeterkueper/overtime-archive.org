# Completion — Mars Fabrication Center canonicalization

Resolved by merged PR #212.

`OTA-TEC-0089-2026-DE` v1.2 now canonizes:
- fabrication as a portfolio of qualified process cells rather than a universal printer;
- separation of manufacturability, feedstock pedigree and release authority;
- P0–P5 part criticality/release classes;
- explicit feedstock states and pedigree;
- reduced early process architecture before advanced powder processes;
- a formal qualification ladder;
- per-cell utility contracts;
- lossy recycling and the explicit ISRU chain through feedstock qualification.

Exact facility power, area, throughput, gas inventory, machine set, mass, local-alloy closure and mission-specific release authority remain open. No NOXIA balancing values were introduced.
