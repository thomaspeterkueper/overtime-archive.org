## Evidence
SOX2 and cMYC direct conversion of human erythroid progenitors to induced neural stem cells without pluripotent intermediate; donors newborn to 101 years. DNAm epigenetic clock readings fell substantially, persisting for weeks, even with inhibited proliferation. Neural identity maturation and methylation-age changes show protracted, partially distinct kinetics. Established lines lack several age-associated cellular hallmarks.

## Guardrails
Distinguish donor chronological age, DNAm clock proxy, cell identity, transcriptional profile, functional phenotype, genomic integrity, safety and clinical outcomes. The headline's 60-year change is not human rejuvenation. Compare matched-cell controls, passages, methylation clock applicability across cell types, batch effects and long-term genomic/tumorigenic risks.
