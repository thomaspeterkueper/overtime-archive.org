# Engineering → OTA: Utopia Industrial Site / I0–I5 Review

KUEPER Engineering has completed the first-order site and industrial validation for the Utopia search corridor.

## Proposed canonicalizable conclusions

- Keep `44–47° N / 105–115° E` as a search corridor rather than a single validated point.
- Do not freeze `45° N / 110° E` as the final settlement coordinate until a local site-survey package passes Engineering gates.
- Separate permanent settlement foundations from active ice-extraction fields unless subsidence and thermal disturbance are explicitly qualified.
- Treat industrial depth I0–I5 as capability/qualification stages, not one self-sufficiency percentage.
- Preserve the full material chain:
  `resource -> beneficiation -> refinement -> composition/alloy control -> qualified feedstock -> manufacturing -> post-processing -> inspection/release -> use`.
- Local structural/mechanical capability should generally mature earlier than advanced electronics, sensors, precision optics and selected high-performance materials/components.
- 'Local machine production' should state the dependency boundary; local frames/housings do not imply local bearings, seals, sensors or power electronics.

## Proposed I0–I5 interpretation

- I0: imported-system operation/basic maintenance.
- I1: repair/simple fabrication/noncritical parts.
- I2: controlled structural/mechanical production with process qualification and metrology.
- I3: selected feedstock conditioning plus broader machine/robot modules.
- I4: integrated local chain for explicitly qualified product/material families.
- I5: diversified industrial autonomy with persistent specialist imports still possible.

## Site gates remaining open

Before final coordinate freeze Engineering requires:
- high-resolution slope/roughness mapping;
- boulder density/size distribution;
- subsurface ice geometry/depth confidence;
- ground-stability/subsidence indicators;
- landing / industrial / habitat zoning;
- route/utility options;
- extraction thermal/subsidence risk model;
- power and heat-rejection geometry.

## Not proposed for canon yet

No production rates, exact safety distances, exact population, exact landing coordinate, exact mine geometry or exact infrastructure spacing are closed.
