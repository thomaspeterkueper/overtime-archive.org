- **Origin:** NOXIA research intake

## Request
Create an evidence-oriented dossier for GJ 3090 b and the broader class of strongly spin-orbit-misaligned/retrograde planets around cool stars.

Verify and preserve primary-source measurements where available: stellar/planet parameters, orbital period, projected spin-orbit angle and measurement method. Separate observation from formation hypotheses.

Evaluate mechanisms including primordial disk misalignment, late/misaligned accretion and secondary disk formation, planet-planet dynamics, stellar companions and migration. Explicitly mark excluded/constrained companions and remaining uncertainty.

Extract reusable constraints for NOXIA procedural planetary-system generation and an educational module on angular momentum, orbital planes, migration, transits, radial velocity and the Rossiter-McLaughlin effect.
