## Anlass

`OTA-SCI-0091-2026-DE` documents the new Supernovae Unite Hubble diagram, which combines Pantheon+ and DES-SN5YR into an internally more consistent sample of 2,884 likely Type Ia supernovae.

The result is relevant to AVI because it tightens empirical constraints on cosmic expansion history. It is **not** evidence for AVI, `xi`, `Phi(a)`, or history dependence at fixed present state.

## Requested AVI action

Add Unite to the AVI observational test matrix as an external distance-redshift likelihood once the model exposes quantitative observables suitable for likelihood comparison.

At minimum AVI should be able to predict or derive:

- `H(a)` / `H(z)`;
- luminosity distance `d_L(z)`;
- distance modulus `mu(z)`;
- an effective `w_eff(a)` only if that mapping is mathematically well-defined and does not replace the underlying AVI dynamics.

## Required comparison discipline

1. Reproduce the published Unite reference fit for Flat-LambdaCDM before testing AVI.
2. Use the same data vector, covariance and systematic-error treatment where available.
3. Compare AVI against both Flat-LambdaCDM and the phenomenological `w0-wa` model.
4. Account for additional AVI degrees of freedom in model comparison.
5. Do not use only the headline sigma preference as evidence.
6. Preserve the distinction between Bayesian evidence and frequentist significance.

## Relation to AVI Test B

Unite should **not** be classified as a positive result for Test B (`Historienseparation bei gleichem Gegenwartszustand`).

The supernova Hubble diagram constrains an integrated expansion history. It does not by itself demonstrate two empirically admissible histories A and B with identical present state `Y(a*)` but different present AVI response.

The useful role is narrower:

> Unite reduces the space of observationally admissible expansion histories from which a genuine Test-B construction may later be built.

## Guardrails

Do not infer from `OTA-SCI-0091` that:

- dark energy has been demonstrated to evolve;
- AVI has received empirical support;
- `Phi(a)` or `xi` has been detected;
- an integrated distance observable is equivalent to physical memory;
- a better fit alone establishes the physical reality of an AVI state variable.

## Acceptance criteria

Done when:

1. Unite is listed as a distinct external observational dataset in the AVI test matrix;
2. its preprint status and systematic uncertainties are visible;
3. the model-to-observable mapping needed for a real likelihood test is explicit;
4. Test B remains separately defined and is not relabelled as passed;
5. future peer-review or independent reanalysis of Unite can update the AVI evidence status without rewriting the theory definition.
