## Request
Review Engineering closure `ENG-SYS-MMC-0001` for canonicalization into the OTA Mars Medical Center dossier.

## Proposed canon candidates
- size medical capability by credible scenarios, treatment duration and logistics latency rather than terrestrial beds-per-capita ratios;
- use scalable capability packages M0–M3 from stabilization through settlement referral capability;
- separate patient, clean-supply and contaminated flows;
- isolation zones require explicit local ventilation/pressure/filtration state and ECLSS interface;
- hybrid blood strategy combining limited stored products with a typed/screened donor registry is the Engineering baseline;
- pharmaceutical management is lot/expiry/storage/substitution/reserve based;
- sterilization/reprocessing is a validated state chain rather than a binary machine cycle;
- staffing is capability- and cross-training-based, including key-person-loss cases;
- autonomous decision support may assist but not silently replace validated medical authority;
- medical failures couple causally to power, water, O2, cold-chain, staff and contamination states through MSSK.

## Remain Engineering/open
Exact beds, floor area, staff headcount, mass, power, water, gas inventories and blood/pharmaceutical reserve quantities remain scenario/equipment outputs until population, risk profile and logistics cadence are fixed. Advanced imaging, pharmaceutical stability and detailed radiation-specific burden remain open research/design items.

OTA remains sole authority for canonical identity/value changes.
