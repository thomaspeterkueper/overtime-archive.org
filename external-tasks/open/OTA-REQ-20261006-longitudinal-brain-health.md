# OTA Request: Longitudinal Brain Health evidence dossier

Origin: research synthesis, 2026-10-06
Priority: high

Create a provenance-first dossier for brain health across the life course. Anchor it in the 2024 Lancet Commission dementia prevention framework and WHO 2026 risk-reduction guidelines, then connect relevant existing archive strands on hearing/vision, cardiometabolic health, physical activity, social connectedness, sleep, voice/language biomarkers and cellular maintenance.

Maintain separate evidence tracks for epidemiological association, intervention evidence, mechanistic evidence and emerging biomarkers. Preserve life-stage, population, geography and equity context. Record population-attributable fractions as population-level estimates rather than individual predictions. Retrospectively index existing related OTA entries and prepare candidate claims for KG review.
