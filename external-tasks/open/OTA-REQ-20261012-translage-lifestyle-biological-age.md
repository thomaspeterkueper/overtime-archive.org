# OTA Research Dossier Request: TranslAGE / Lifestyle–Biological Age Coupling

Date: 2026-10-12
Status: evidence intake, primary source identified; full study-level extraction pending.

## Provenance
Media trigger: https://www.focus.de/gesundheit/news/mittelmeerdiaet-und-bewegung-verringern-das-biologische-alter_c1f29134-d0a5-48b1-a362-b976b56f0233.html
Primary: Sehgal et al., "Responsiveness of epigenetic aging biomarkers to longevity interventions in humans", Nature Medicine, published 2026-08-21, DOI: 10.1038/s41591-026-04562-9.
Primary URL: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-026-04562-9
Dataset: TranslAGE, 51 longitudinal intervention studies, 3,128 blood samples, 16 epigenetic clocks and 94 additional DNAm biomarkers.

## Supported findings
Intervention responses differ by clock, population and duration. 19 interventions showed nominal reductions across clocks, 13 after multiple-testing correction. Two Mediterranean-diet interventions showed concordant reductions in subsets of generation-2+ clocks. Pharmacological interventions showed larger average changes; these findings do not establish clinical benefit from prescribing medications to healthy people. Supplements showed less consistent results.

## Interpretation gates
Biomarker responsiveness != proven reversal of organismal aging != demonstrated improved healthspan/lifespan.
Record clock generation, direction, standardized effect size, multiple-testing adjustment, sample population, comparator, study duration, and clinical outcomes separately.
A medication effect in an ill population cannot be generalized to preventive use in healthy individuals.
PREDIMED-Plus body-composition findings are separate evidence and must not be substituted for TranslAGE findings.

## Research actions
1. Extract exact diet/exercise arms and effect sizes from primary supplement tables.
2. Review cohort comparability, batch effects, pre/post designs and heterogeneity.
3. Map DunedinPACE, PCGrimAge, SystemsAge and organ-specific DNAm scores.
4. Link Dynamic Cellular Maintenance, Longitudinal Brain Health, Biological Robustness Engineering and longitudinal measurement.
5. Revisit whether clock changes predict functional/clinical outcomes; avoid consumer age-reversal claims.

Review interval: 12 months or earlier upon meaningful clinical validation.
