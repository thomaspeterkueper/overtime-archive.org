# OTA Request: Cellular maintenance and autophagy evidence dossier

Origin: research intake, 2026-10-06
Priority: high

Build a provenance-first literature dossier on age-related cellular maintenance. Start with the current human autophagy-flux work and the recent CMA/senescence work, then connect older evidence on autophagy, lysosomes, mitophagy, proteostasis and senescent-cell clearance.

Track separately:
- direct functional/flux measurements
- gene/transcript/protein proxies
- human observational evidence
- animal/mechanistic evidence
- intervention evidence

Record cell type, sex, age and physiological context wherever available. Explicitly test the competing interpretations “higher maintenance activity = better maintenance” versus “higher activity = response to higher damage burden.” Link earlier archive entries rather than duplicating them and prepare claims for KG review.
