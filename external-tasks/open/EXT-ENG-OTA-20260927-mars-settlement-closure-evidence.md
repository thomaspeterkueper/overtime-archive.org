# EXTERNAL TASK — Mars settlement closure & resilience evidence base

- **Origin:** Integrated Mars Settlement Systems Kernel
- **Target:** OTA
- **Date:** 2026-09-27
- **Status:** open

## Request
Build/extend the evidence base needed to calibrate an integrated Mars settlement model.

Prioritize primary/technical sources for:
- ISS/ECLSS water and atmosphere recovery;
- controlled-environment agriculture and crop resource demands;
- bioregenerative life-support research;
- nutrient recovery and waste processing;
- Mars water/regolith constraints and ISRU;
- power/storage under Mars environmental conditions;
- maintenance/spares/reliability for isolated systems;
- robotic inspection/maintenance;
- closed ecological-system experiments and failure lessons.

For each claim preserve technology readiness, environment, scale, duration and whether results are measured, modeled or projected.

Explicitly identify where a gameplay simplification would otherwise violate mass/energy conservation or imply unrealistic self-sufficiency.
