# COSMO-PRIOR-01 / COSMO-COV-01

Sources: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26493 and https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26097

Preprints, 19 October 2026. Prior and covariance sensitivity are standard-model controls; AVI-P T1–T4 inactive.

## Results
Layton et al.: switching to Jeffreys priors reverses Bayesian model preference in some DESI DR2/CMB/SN combinations, while w0/wa posteriors shift little. Hu et al.: shared 38x38 cluster covariance, matching, and calibration make an apparent curvature offset unsuitable as evidence for nonzero cosmic curvature.

## Null controls
Treat prior sensitivity, shared covariance, and calibration as standard explanations before considering any cross-channel residual. T1-T4 remain inactive. No experimental verification or independent replication established.
