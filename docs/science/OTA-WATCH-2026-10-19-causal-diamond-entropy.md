# Dynamical entropy of causal diamonds

Source: Chakraborty, Das, Mahato, arXiv:2610.22398, submitted 14 October 2026; preprint, not independently confirmed. https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22398

Theoretical result: A Wald-Zoupas charge yields a cut-dependent entropy with area and expansion terms. At the diamond edge it reduces to area over four in natural units. An exact signed flux balance follows in Einstein gravity. Local monotonicity requires positivity of the relevant total flux; the null energy condition alone is insufficient. A linearized first law does not require a background Killing symmetry. The semiclassical extension depends on an improved quantum null energy condition and its assumptions.

Standard baseline: Einstein gravity, Raychaudhuri equation, covariant phase space, and conditional semiclassical energy inequalities. No independent experiment.
